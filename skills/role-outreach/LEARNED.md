- **2026-10-09** — Verify a saved draft's attachments inside the Gmail tab, not with get_draft: FULL_CONTENT and PLAIN_TEXT leave attachments out, and RAW returns the whole MIME (about 900 KB of base64 for three PDFs). Fetch /mail/u/N/?ik=<GLOBALS[9]>&view=om&permmsgid=msg-a:<draft id> for the part list and order (a draft's Show original keeps each attachment's headers but empties its body; take the <pre id=raw_message_text> by regex, because Trusted Types block DOMParser), then fetch ?ui=2&ik=...&attid=0.K&permmsgid=msg-a:<draft id>&view=att&disp=safe for each K and compare its SHA-256 with the local file in the page, returning only the match. Wait about 5 s after Save & close, or the last attachment can come back as a 6 KB error page. Gmail replaces the compose's file input after every upload, so find it again before each file_upload.

- **2026-10-09** — To write a reply body in Gmail by script, focus div[aria-label='Message Body'] and call document.execCommand insertText and insertParagraph (two insertParagraph calls make his blank line); insertHTML is refused under Trusted Types.
