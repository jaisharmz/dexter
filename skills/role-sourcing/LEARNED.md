# Learned — role-sourcing

Corrections this skill has earned in use. Read before running it; these override
`SKILL.md`. Protocol: `kernel/skill-learning.md`.

- **2026-09-28** — Never credit the operator with a library their work only builds on. Name whose library it is and say exactly which part is theirs. A wrong ownership claim spreads into every thesis that reuses the artifact, so correct it at the source in `config/persona.md`.
- **2026-09-28** — Read job boards through the ATS JSON feeds (curl) and company pages through WebFetch before spending any WebSearch: the 200-call WebSearch budget is shared by every agent in the session, and a six-batch parallel sweep exhausted it mid-run, leaving LinkedIn snippet reads and funding checks undone. Save WebSearch for LinkedIn snippets and funding news.
- **2026-09-28** — Ask the operator for the LinkedIn People-tab read on every company before handing over a list to apply to. A sweep once skipped the read to save search budget and the operator asked for it anyway: the read dropped two companies on low hit rates and surfaced a warm path to a co-founder. It costs the operator one page load per company, in their own signed-in browser, and costs no WebSearch, so the budget is never a reason to skip it.
- **2026-09-30** — A seniority filter on titles must not match the 'staff' in 'Member of Technical Staff': a 2026-09-30 sweep dropped every MTS req that way until fixed, hiding new-grad-open roles at Fireworks, Preference Model, Reflection, Rhoda, Moonlake, Odyssey and Radical Numerics.
- **2026-09-30** — Years-of-experience grep must also catch spelled-out counts ('two years', 'five or more years'), and only degree or years gates outside a Preferred/Nice-to-have section exclude the operator (2026-09-30 Simplify sweep).
- **2026-09-30** — Verify an ATS slug by the board's own website, not its name alone: Ashby exposes organization.publicWebsite in the __appData of jobs.ashbyhq.com/<slug>, and it must match the company domain. On 2026-09-30 name-only probes matched Runway to cfo.ai, Circle to circle.so, Warp to warp.co, Flock Safety to flockcover.com and Moonshot AI to a power-equipment firm.
- **2026-09-30** — Forms can be pre-screened without a browser on more ATSs than Greenhouse and Ashby: Gem lists jobs at api.gem.com/job_board/v0/<slug>/job_posts/ and returns fields and questions from a POST to jobs.gem.com/api/public/graphql (oatsJobPostFieldsAndQuestions, jobBoardVanityPath + jobPostExtId); Workable returns its form at apply.workable.com/api/v1/jobs/<shortcode>/form. Ashby's non-user-graphql returns 429 above about 4 concurrent requests, so never cache a 429 and retry with backoff (2026-09-30).
- **2026-09-30** — Count a years-of-experience line as preferred only when it sits under a Preferred/Nice-to-have/Bonus header line or its own bullet says so. On 2026-09-30 a 'PhD strongly preferred' bullet earlier in the list made World Labs' '6+ years' requirement read as optional.
- **2026-09-30** — For the LinkedIn People-tab read: resolve every slug through LinkedIn company search and check the follower count before reading, because 15 of 60 guessed slugs on 2026-09-30 were decoys or dead (worldlabs, primeintellect, cognition-ai, figureai, luma-ai, reflection-ai, 1xtech, anduril-industries, simile-ai and others; confirmed slugs are in state/linkedin-scorecard-2026-09-30.md). Wait about 3 s and scroll to the bottom twice with javascript_tool before get_page_text: reading straight after navigate returns the previous page, and two scrolls load about 70 cards. Above about 200 members the first 15 headlines are bare, so report a stretched ratio from those 70 cards next to the 15-profile one, and call out 'Founding …' title inflation where a company hands it out widely.
- **2026-09-30** — For the LinkedIn People-tab read, confirm a slug by the website on its About page, not by follower count alone: for small or new companies the decoy can out-follow the real page (2026-09-30: 'ZYPHRA TECH' 5.7K and 'ZyphraTech' 4.3K against the real zyphra at 4K; 'Tomo Labs' at tomoai 4K against the real Tomo at tomodotai, 778). Claude in Chrome's find on a companies search-results page returns each result's href, so the slug comes from one call.
- **2026-09-30** — In the LinkedIn People-tab read, score a degree or school line with no achievement as uninformative, not a miss, and count a PhD as a hit only when the university is named; employer lists ('ex-X', 'prev @ Y') are the misses. The tab orders cards by the viewer's network, so at a large or foreign company the first 70 cards are the viewer's US 2nd-degree cohort rather than the core team (Mistral, 2026-09-30: 840 of 1,751 members in France, almost none surfaced); say so next to the ratio. A generic company name (Factory, Captions, Ollama) pulls unrelated people into the member facets, so discount the function and location split there.
- **2026-09-30** — X is a sourcing channel now (the operator's instruction, 2026-09-30): the daily-briefing skill appends companies hiring, launching or raising in the operator's areas, and the people behind them, to state/lists/x-leads.jsonl every morning, each with the post that shows it. - **2026-10-01** — VC portfolio boards read without a browser: Consider boards (jobs.kleinerperkins.com, jobs.sequoiacap.com, jobs.lsvp.com, careers.nea.com) take POST /api-boards/search-jobs with the csrfToken from the /jobs HTML sent as x-csrf-token plus that page's session cookie, body {meta:{size:100,sequence}, board:{id,isParent}, query:{markets:['AI','Automation & Robotics'], jobTypes:[...], jobSeniority:['junior']}}; Getro boards (Khosla collection 257, General Catalyst 222) take POST api.getro.com/api/v2/collections/<id>/search/jobs with filters as a hash ({seniority:['entry_level']}) and return at most 20 per page; Greylock server-renders 60 jobs per page at greylock.com/jobs/portfolio-jobs/?page=N. jobs.foundersfund.com did not resolve and a16z and Index are client-rendered (2026-10-01 AI-startup sweep).
- **2026-10-01** — Ashby's ApiJobPosting needs the decoded board name ('Spectral Labs', not 'Spectral%20Labs') or jobPosting comes back null. Split multi-city locations ('London & San Francisco') before the non-US check and treat 'Remote (EMEA/East Coast)' as a US option (Apollo Research and Poolside were wrongly dropped). '(Internship and Full-time)' titles are not intern-only (Tilde). - **2026-10-01** — Loose exclusion matching by name prefix produces false hits that hide real companies, such as a robotics startup read as an unrelated company that shares its first word, or an AI startup read as a trading firm whose name is its prefix. Confirm every hit on the full name or domain before dropping a company; gate.py prints the logged company each hit matched (2026-10-01 additional-queue sweep).
